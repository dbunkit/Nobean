Any trademark considerations?

A prior U.S. application for the exact term "NOBEAN" (filed 2022, unrelated party, nut-snack category) was abandoned and is no longer active per USPTO records. Buyers should independently verify current status and run their own clearance search before committing to brand around this name, since trademark records change. Two adjacent names are worth knowing about: an existing mushroom-coffee brand called "Neobean," and a live U.S. application for "NOBEANA" in the coffee category (status: published, under examination as of this listing). Neither prevents ownership or resale of this domain, but either could be relevant if a buyer intends to build a brand called exactly "Nobean" rather than use the domain for another purpose. This isn't legal advice; buyers should consult their own counsel.
